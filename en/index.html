About the forum

One day to turn industrial needs into joint production

The forum brings German manufacturers and technology suppliers together with Ukrainian defence companies, investors and practitioners. Focus on Build with Ukraine: joint production, technology transfer and concrete industrial partnerships, not just declarations.

01

Joint production

Joint ventures, licensing and practical conditions for producing together.

02

Pre-arranged B2B meetings

Matchmaking between German and Ukrainian companies, agreed before the event.

03

Investment

Capital and scaling opportunities for Ukrainian defence-tech companies.

Who should attend

Built for four groups

Ukrainian companies

Present capabilities and needs, find German production and supply partners.

German industry

Manufacturers and tech suppliers looking for partners, field-proven technology and new markets.

Investors

Access to a curated pipeline of Ukrainian defence-tech companies ready to scale.

Defence sector

Practitioners and institutions shaping requirements and cooperation frameworks.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Early Bird, Regular and Late?

Only the price. It is the same ticket with the same access; the price depends on when you buy.

Will I receive an invoice for my company?

Yes. Select “Invoice for a company” at checkout and enter the company name, billing address and VAT ID.

Which payment methods are accepted?

Credit and debit cards. Payment by bank transfer against invoice is available for companies.

Can I buy tickets for colleagues?

Yes. You can buy several tickets in one order and enter the details of each attendee.

Can I transfer my ticket to a colleague?

Please contact the organising team with your order number and the new attendee's details.

What is the cancellation policy?

Terms of cancellation and refunds are set out in the terms of sale.

What language is the forum held in?

English. Details on interpretation will follow.

Questions?

The organising team will get back to you within one business day.
